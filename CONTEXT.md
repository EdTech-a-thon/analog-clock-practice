# Clock Literacy

A practice tool for students learning to read an analog clock. A student works
through short, self-selected rounds and can carry a printed summary back to
their teacher.

## Language

**Round**:
A run of Questions at one Level, ending in a results screen. The student picks
its length before starting: 5, 10, or 20 Questions, ten by default.
_Avoid_: quiz, game, session, set

**Question**:
One prompt and its answer, in one Direction, at the Round's Level.
_Avoid_: problem, item, card, exercise

**Level**:
How hard the Questions are: Easy, Medium, or Hard. Chosen by the student before
a Round and fixed for that Round.
_Avoid_: difficulty, mode, tier, grade

**Direction**:
Which way a Question runs. Clock-to-Time shows a Clock Face and asks for the
time; Time-to-Clock shows a time and asks which Clock Face matches.
_Avoid_: mode, format, question type

**Clock Face**:
A rendered analog clock: the twelve hour numbers, sixty minute ticks, and both
hands. Identical at every Level — the Level changes the times asked and how the
answer is given, never the dial itself.
_Avoid_: clock, dial, watch

**Distractor**:
A wrong answer offered alongside the right one.
_Avoid_: decoy, wrong option, fake answer

**Hour Slip**:
The Distractor that names the hour the minute hand is approaching rather than
the one it has left — 4:45 for a clock reading 3:45. The misconception the tool
exists to correct.
_Avoid_: off-by-one, hour error

**Meridiem**:
Whether a Question's time is AM or PM. Shown above and to the right of the Clock
Face so that every time a student reads carries one. Context only: it is never
entered, never read off the dial, and no Question depends on it.
_Avoid_: period (a period is a slot in the school day), am/pm, time of day

**Practice Report**:
The printable summary a student takes to their teacher, covering the Round they
just finished and carrying the name they typed.
_Avoid_: PDF, export, certificate, results
