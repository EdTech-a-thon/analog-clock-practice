# Hard Time-to-Clock offers four clocks rather than draggable hands

At the Hard Level, Clock-to-Time asks the student to type the time, so the
symmetric choice for Time-to-Clock would be to hand them a blank Clock Face and
have them drag the hands into position. We rejected that and kept Time-to-Clock
as a four-way choice at every Level, tightening the Distractors instead.

## Considered Options

Dragging the hands is the only exercise in the tool a student cannot guess their
way through, and it is the true inverse of typing a time. It was rejected on
cost and access: it is the largest single piece of work in the project, and a
drag interaction needs a full keyboard alternative before it is usable by every
student. Four clocks with an Hour Slip in the pool tests recognition of the same
misconception at a fraction of the cost.
